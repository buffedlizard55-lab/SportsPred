How to read a Rugby League card

WIN MATCH ≥70 HIGH · 50–69 MEDIUM · below 50 SKIP (at least two factors must align for 50–69 to clear MEDIUM).

HANDICAP — only when WIN MATCH ≥65, line inside −5.50 to −8.50 for favourites or +6.50 to +13.50 for underdogs, and average winning margin ≥8. Thresholds: ≥70 HIGH · 55–69 MEDIUM · below 55 SKIP. Shows only who will cover, never the line.

GAME TOTAL — primary window 42.50–48.50 is Over only (≥55 HIGH · 45–54 MEDIUM · below 45 SKIP). Above that: 49.50–52.50 and 53.50+ require stricter offensive/defensive proof; rain or strong wind elevates Under by 10 points automatically. Shows only Over or Under, never the total.

Every card is capped at six active selections per day across all three markets; excess leans are withheld as SKIP. Open Analysis on any fixture to see every rule that fired, its points and the factors that were not available. Full register: sources.
