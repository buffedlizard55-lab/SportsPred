# Rugby League irregularities — IR-RUGBY-01 … IR-RUGBY-07

| ID | Title | Status | Detail | Impact on scoring | Writer handling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IR-RUGBY-01 | No free key-less consolidated odds feed | **OPEN** | OLBG publishes tipster consensus percentages but no structured bookmaker odds API. ESPN rugby-league publishes no price. The only public odds-like data are server-rendered handicap/total selection strings on OLBG event pages (e.g. `+10.50`). | `odds.moneyline` is derived from ladder rank (rank 1 → 1.65, top 3 → 1.85, rest → 2.40) and recorded as *ladder-implied*; the engine scores `odds_value` from this derived price and the writer never mentions a figure. `missing[]` lists the cross-bookmaker requirement where not met. | No digit, no odds figure and no price is ever printed; validator rejects any numeral. |
| IR-RUGBY-02 | No free key-less per-match results tape for last 5 | **OPEN** | The official NRL and Super League sites are JS-hydrated archives with no public key-less results endpoint that returns the full last-5 W/L plus margins key-less. The last-5 pattern and `avgWinningMargin` are therefore seeded deterministically from rank (top teams W-heavy, bottom L-heavy) and listed as *estimated* in provenance. | `form.last5` and `margin.avgWinningMargin` contribute at most 25 + 8 pts, but both carry a `missing[]` penalty if the tape is not fetched live; the ceiling drops by 5 per missing factor. | Step 4 output refers only to general forward dominance / defensive structure, never to a named margin. |
| IR-RUGBY-03 | Cross-reference of ≥2 bookmakers | **OPEN** | The prompt asks to cross-check moneyline, handicap and total from ≥2 sources with line movement. No free consolidated feed provides two independent bookmaker identities plus movement. OLBG lists a single handicap line and a single total line per event where priced. | Recorded as missing (`odds.cross_ref`, `handicapLine.cross_ref`, `totalLine.cross_ref`); the engine never blocks scoring on it but the flag is shown in the analysis panel. | Not mentioned in tips. |
| IR-RUGBY-04 | Weather at venue | **PARTIAL** | Open-Meteo returns dry clear for Sydney / Brisbane / Leeds on 2026-09-02 snapshot; the committed `rugby_league_weather.json` stores `isClear:true` for every match. A live fetch by lat/lon is wired but the snapshot window has no rain/strong-wind case to exercise the 10-pt Under elevation. | `weather.isClear` adds 15 pts to Over (or 5 neutral if light rain, 20 to Under if heavy rain/strong wind); the `heavyRain/strongWind` +10 to Under rule is implemented and tested but not triggered on this window. | Weather note appears only when rain/wind is forecast. |
| IR-RUGBY-05 | OLBG handicap/total lines are selection strings, not decimal fields | **OPEN** | OLBG prints handicap and total as selection labels (`Brisbane Broncos +10.50`, `Over 53.50`) rather than a numeric `line` field. The parser extracts the numeric suffix with regex and stores it as `handicap_lines`/`total_lines`. | Engine reads the parsed `line` when present; when an event is unpriced the market is scored as `missing` and the tip is withheld as SKIP. | Writer prints only `to cover` or `Over`/`Under`, never the line. |
| IR-RUGBY-06 | Super League official standings are truncated | **OPEN** | `superleague.co.uk/standings` sometimes truncates PF/PA totals for mid-table clubs. Wikipedia's `2026_Super_League_season` 2026-08-28 snapshot is the secondary source that provides complete PF/PA for all 14 clubs. Both sources are cited in provenance. | `teams.json` PF/PA are the Wikipedia row where the official count is truncated; rank order is identical, so `attackRank`/`defenseRank` are stable. | No figures are printed. |
| IR-RUGBY-07 | State of Origin outright vs match market | **ACKNOWLEDGED** | OLBG lists `State of Origin 2027 — Win Tournament` (event_id 2695857) among Rugby League events. It has no `home`/`away` and no handicap/total line. | The engine excludes `type:outright` from scoring (`scoreRugbyLeagueCard` only iterates `matches.json` fixtures); the slate retains it for calendar completeness and the validator exempts it from home/away requirements. | Never appears on the rugby league scoreboard (matches only). |

`docs/IRREGULARITIES.md` and `docs/SOURCES.md` summarise the same items cross-sport; per-sport detail lives here. Every irregularity is cited in `data/rugby_league_provenance.json`.
