`data/nrl_matches.json`. Committed as `data/nrl_backtest.json`.

## Window

- Rounds 7-27, 2026-04-16 to 2026-09-03
- 149 fixtures scored
- Rounds 1-6 are warm-up: a club needs a form sample behind it before any factor
  can be measured.

## Method

1. Walk-forward: every fixture is scored only from matches with an earlier date, with the ladder, form, byes, head-to-head and reference total all recomputed at that point.
2. First round scored: 7, so that every club has a form sample behind it. Rounds 1-6 are warm-up and are not counted.
3. No prices are used anywhere, so no return on investment is reported. Only strike rates against the actual result.
4. The weather factor is excluded from the model for the backtest (no free historical forecast is committed), so the backtested engine carries ten fewer points than the live card.

## Settlement rules

- **WIN MATCH:** Correct if the picked club won the match. A drawn match counts as a miss, because the engine does not publish draws.
- **GAME TOTAL:** Settled against the rolling season mean total the engine would have used on the day, not against a market line. Reported separately and clearly labelled.
- **HANDICAP:** Not settled. No free source publishes historical NRL handicap lines, so publishing a strike rate would require inventing the line.

## Reading the numbers honestly

- The WIN MATCH strike rate is a *selection* strike rate over one season of 131
  published picks. It is not a claim about profit: most of these picks are
  favourites, and a favourite-heavy card can lose money at a strike rate like this.
- The GAME TOTAL figure is close to a coin flip. That is a real result and it is
  published rather than tuned away: the market sets the total, and this model
  has no price to compare against.
- The backtested engine carries one fewer factor than the live card (no
  weather), so the live card is not strictly the thing that was measured.
