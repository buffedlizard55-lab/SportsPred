How to read an NPB card

Each side is scored on recent form (25 pts), starting pitcher form (25 — quality starts, not season ERA), run differential (20), odds and value (20) and same-league head-to-head (10). Independently, every match gets a draw likelihood score: two strong starters (30), two fresh bullpens (25), a run-differential gap under one run (20), a draw rate above the league norm (15) and close, low-scoring recent meetings (10). When the draw score reaches 65 and the two sides sit within 10 points, the draw becomes the primary pick — a genuine selection, never a hedge.

The run line is only considered when the favourite's win score is 60 or more and the draw score is under 55; the total scores an Over ledger against an Under ledger, with rain at an open-air park in the rainy or typhoon season pushing Under and enclosed venues scoring no weather at all. Every market must clear its Step 3 gate and a minimum share of sourced evidence before a tip is written; otherwise it is a SKIP with the reason shown.
