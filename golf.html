How to read a golf card

Outright ≥75 HIGH · 60–74 MEDIUM · below 60 LOW (value play, never skipped). Top six ≥65 HIGH · 55–64 MEDIUM · below 55 SKIP. First round leader ≥65 MEDIUM, HIGH only at ≥75 with an early tee ahead of worsening weather. Regional ≥70 HIGH · 55–69 MEDIUM · below 55 or within five points of a rival LOW.

A market whose core evidence is not published (strokes gained, course history, ranking) can never read HIGH. Open Analysis on any tournament to see every rule that fired, its points, the factors that were not available and the source links.

Amateurs are excluded. Odds are not available from any free source, so value flags use world-ranking position within the field. Full register: sources.
