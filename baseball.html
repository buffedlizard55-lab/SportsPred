How to read a baseball card

Each side is scored on recent form (25 pts), starting pitcher quality (25), run differential (20), odds and value (20) and head-to-head (10). The run line reuses that base with the head-to-head block swapped for run-margin analysis, plus starter dominance, bullpen, run differential and fatigue modifiers. The total scores an Over ledger and an Under ledger across offence, starter run suppression, bullpen and weather.

Before any score becomes a pick it must clear the Step 3 gates. A market below its threshold becomes a SKIP with the reason shown. Open Analysis on any match for every rule that fired, its points, the factors that could not be sourced and the official review links.
