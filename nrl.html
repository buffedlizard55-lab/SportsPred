How to read an NRL card

WIN MATCH ≥70 HIGH · 50–69 with two or more factors aligned MEDIUM · below 50 SKIP.

HANDICAP — live only when WIN MATCH is 60 or higher, then ≥70 HIGH · 55–69 MEDIUM · below 55 SKIP. Only the side that covers is ever named, never the number.

GAME TOTAL — a directional advantage of 20 points or more is HIGH, 15–19 MEDIUM, below 15 SKIP. Only Over or Under is ever published, never the total.

At most two markets are published per fixture, and the handicap is withheld unless the side's recent winning margins actually support a cover. Confidence is capped by how much of the model could be sourced: the NRL has no free key-less price feed, so the odds factor always scores zero and is named on every card.
