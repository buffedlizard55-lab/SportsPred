How to read a T20 Blast card

Each side is scored on six declared factors: recent form (30), season points rate (20), head-to-head (15), the league's own measured home-win rate (15), winning-margin profile (10) and rest between fixtures (10). The weights are declared in engine/t20_blast_engine.js and were never fitted against the tape they are validated on.

Everything is walk-forward: a fixture is scored using only results dated strictly before it, so the card you read for a past date is the card the model would have produced that morning.

A market is written only when it clears its gate. Otherwise it is a SKIP with the reason shown — a withheld market is an honest output, not a failure. The three player markets need a confirmed line-up and rolling individual figures, which only exist for a live fixture, so they are withheld across the historical tape.

Open Analysis on any fixture for every factor that fired, its points, what could not be sourced, the caps applied, and the scorecard links to check each figure by hand.
