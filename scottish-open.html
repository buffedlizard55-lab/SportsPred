How this ruleset scores

WIN TOURNAMENT (100) — all-around ball-striking 25 · recent form 20 · wind and links proxy 20 · shot-shaping and short game 20 · world ranking and pedigree 15. ≥75 HIGH · 60–74 MEDIUM · below 60 LOW (never skipped).

FIRST ROUND LEADER (100) — opening-round scoring 30 · tee-time wave 30 · putting 20 · fast start 20. Below 50 SKIP · ≥65 MEDIUM at most · HIGH only at ≥75 with a confirmed wave advantage.

TOP AMERICAN / TOP EUROPEAN / TOP GB AND IRELAND — the win base plus regional modifiers. ≥70 HIGH · 55–69 MEDIUM · below 55 or within five points of a rival LOW.

A factor no free source publishes scores zero, is marked missing and caps the market at MEDIUM. Ball flight, Race to Dubai standings, travel intent, odds and per-round wind are all recorded as missing rather than guessed.
