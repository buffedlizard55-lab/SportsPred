How to read a GAA card

Each side is scored on odds (30 pts), form (25 + bonuses), head-to-head (20), championship pedigree (15) and stage/venue (10), exactly as the GAA master prompt specifies. Draws are flagged when form and meetings sit even.

OLBG publishes tipster votes but no prices, so live bets resolve to SKIP. Club championships without a results tape carry an italic data-gap note.
