What this site does

Collects fixtures, live scores, results, team records, recent form and market prices from ESPN's public, key-less JSON API.

Measures each league's home-win rate, draw rate and scoring mean from completed matches — no home-advantage constant is assumed anywhere.

Scores every match against those signals plus the de-vigged market price, and reports each rule that fired with its source.

Writes a copy-ready prediction, and refuses to publish one when the evidence is too thin.

The honesty rule

A factor with no source is never estimated. It is listed in the match's not available panel and the confidence ceiling drops.

A match with fewer than two sourced signals is marked SKIP rather than given a plausible-looking guess.

Sports with no key-less statistics feed — horse racing, greyhounds, darts, snooker, GAA, cycling — are listed for manual review and never predicted.
