What this site does

Collects fixtures, live scores, results, team records, recent form and market prices from ESPN's public, key-less JSON API.

Measures each league's home-win rate, draw rate and scoring mean from completed matches — no home-advantage constant is assumed anywhere.

Scores every match against those signals plus the de-vigged market price, and reports each rule that fired with its source.

Writes a copy-ready prediction, and refuses to publish one when the evidence is too thin.

The honesty rule

A factor with no source is never estimated. It is listed in the match's not available panel and the confidence ceiling drops.

A match with fewer than two sourced signals is marked SKIP rather than given a plausible-looking guess.

Greyhounds run on the official GBGB results database on their own greyhound scoreboard. Volleyball runs on its own scoreboard: NCAA from ESPN, EuroVolley Women from a committed CEV tape — the two families are never mixed. Snooker runs on its own snooker scoreboard: the OLBG market slate joined to the official WST ranking table and the public snooker.org results database, with the odds factor honestly recorded as missing because no free key-less price feed exists. Darts runs on its own darts scoreboard: the OLBG market slate joined to the public PDC Order of Merit and a committed European Tour results tape; the odds factor is recorded as missing because no free key-less price feed exists, and unpublished pairings (Czech Darts Open) are never invented. GAA (football and hurling) runs on its own GAA scoreboard: the OLBG club-championship slate plus printed All-Ireland scorelines; odds are recorded as missing because no free key-less price feed exists. Sports with no key-less statistics feed — horse racing, cycling — are listed for manual review and never predicted.
