How to read a darts card

Each player is scored on odds strength (25 pts), recent form (20 pts + 5 in-tournament bonus), three-dart average (20 pts), head-to-head weighted to the last three years (15 pts), PDC Order of Merit (10 pts, −5 when the opponent is ranked higher) and tournament stage (10 pts), exactly as the darts master prompt specifies.

The public OLBG darts pages publish tipster vote counts but no prices, and no free key-less odds feed exists for darts, so the odds component is recorded as missing and Step 3 returns SKIP on live cards — HIGH confidence is impossible without a measured price. Every written prediction is still produced, with the verdict and the sourced reasons behind it.
