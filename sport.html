How to read a card

HIGH 72+ · MEDIUM 58–71 · LOW 46–57 · SKIP below that or not enough sourced evidence.

Confidence is capped when the league baseline could not be measured, when no price is published, or when fewer than three independent signals fired. A capped card can never read HIGH.
