Format — read this first

Two events share one name. The ranking edition puts 128 players into 32 groups of four each summer and plays best of four frames, so a match can finish 2-2 — three points for a win, one for a draw, and the fourth frame is skipped at 3-0. The invitational edition uses groups of seven and best of five, which cannot draw.

The engine refuses to score until the edition is stated, and the correct-score outcome set switches with it: 3-0 / 3-1 / 2-2 for the ranking edition, 3-0 / 3-1 / 3-2 for the invitational.

Group placings can turn on highest break when points and frame difference are level, so break-building form has a direct, checkable influence beyond who wins each match.
