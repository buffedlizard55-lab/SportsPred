Card rules

MATCH WINNER is scored from recent VNL form, meeting history, confirmed squad status, two-book price context and standings stakes.

SET SCORE selects one margin only: 3-0, 3-1 or 3-2 for the favoured side. Close or incomplete evidence is a SKIP.

OLBG votes are displayed for review only. They are not odds and do not influence a score.
