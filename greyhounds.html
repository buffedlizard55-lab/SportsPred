How to read a greyhound card

Each runner is scored on recent form, trap-draw record, distance fit and track/grade context exactly as the greyhound master prompt specifies, with the last two runs weighted most heavily.

Selections need 55 points or more, at least two placed runs in the last five, and two measured core categories. Live win odds are not published by any free key-less feed, so the odds component is recorded as missing and confidence on a live card is capped at MEDIUM; HIGH is only reachable in backtests that use the official starting price.
