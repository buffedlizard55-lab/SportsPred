How to read an ice hockey card

Each side is scored on recent form (25 pts), odds and value (25), goaltending (20), offensive and defensive structure (20) and home context (10). The puck line reuses that base with the home block swapped for a puck line cover trend, plus margin, injury, power play and fatigue modifiers. The total scores an Over ledger and an Under ledger across offence, goaltending, special teams and recent trends.

Before any score becomes a pick it passes the subagent layer: two reasoning paths are blended into a consensus, compared with the de-vigged price, and vetoed when the edge is under three points, when the two paths disagree violently, or when the starting goaltender is not confirmed.
